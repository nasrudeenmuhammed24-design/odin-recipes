Noodle recipe

Description

this is a delicious noodle and it is easy to made

it will only take you only 10 min to cook

Ingredients

  • 1 pack of Indomie Noodles
  • 2 cups of water
  • 1 egg
  • Small onion
  • Pepper and oil

Steps

  1. Boil water for pot
  2. Add noodles and spice
  3. Cut onion put inside
  4. Fry egg join am
  5. Serve hot and enjoy
