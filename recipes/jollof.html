Nigerian Jollof Rice

Description

This na the best party Jollof rice wey everybody dey love. E get that smoky party taste and e dey very delicious.

If you cook am well, everybody go ask for second plate.

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of rice
  • 5 fresh tomatoes
  • 3 tatashe (red bell pepper)
  • 2 onions
  • Chicken or meat
  • Vegetable oil
  • Curry, thyme, salt and seasoning cubes

Steps

  1. Blend tomatoes, tatashe and one onion
  2. Boil rice small and wash am
  3. Fry the tomato sauce for oil
  4. Add water, curry, thyme, seasoning
  5. Put rice inside sauce make e cook
  6. Add fried chicken on top and serve
