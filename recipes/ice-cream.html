Vanilla Ice Cream

Description

This na soft and creamy vanilla ice cream. You no need machine, just freezer and e go sweet die!

Perfect for hot afternoon for Lagos. Children go love am well well.

Ingredients

  • 2 cups of whipping cream
  • 1 cup of condensed milk
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla flavor
  • Small chocolate chips (optional)

Steps

  1. Whip the cream till e thick well
  2. Add condensed milk and vanilla, mix am gently
  3. Add chocolate chips if you want
  4. Pour am inside container, cover am
  5. Put for freezer for 6 hours
  6. Scoop and enjoy!
